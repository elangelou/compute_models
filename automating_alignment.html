Why Automate Alignment? Weighing the Tradeoffs
Why do researchers and funders bet on AI doing our homework — and is this a good or bad idea? Arguments for: speed of progress, scale of search space. Arguments against: alignment tax, value lock-in, premature automation.

03

Open Questions Moving Forward

Algorithm improvements: How useful are current models for identifying novel algorithm improvements in AI and alignment research?
Complex codebases: How useful are models in helping improve complicated codebases, especially within AI companies where context depth is critical?
Productivity tracking: How do we better measure the productivity of engineers and scientists? Self-reported data are unreliable — what objective proxies exist?
Task horizon trends (METR): Do findings from the METR task horizon paper continue under current model scaling? Are the trends holding or diverging?
Threat vector tracking: As AI R&D capability improves, what metrics should we use to track the emergence of new threat vectors?
Benchmarks for automating alignment: What would meaningful benchmarks for automating alignment research look like? How do we avoid Goodhart's Law?
